About me

My name is Joana Figueiredo, I'm 29 years old and I'm a visual artist and image designer based in Porto, Portugal.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts (2019) and a Master's degree in Image Design (2022), both from the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto.

My practice explores the intersection of art, image and visual expression, often incorporating poetic and symbolic elements into my work. I am particularly interested in the ways images can communicate ideas, evoke emotions and create meaningful connections.

I am creative, curious and driven, approaching each project as an opportunity to explore new perspectives, experiment with different approaches and continuously develop my practice.
